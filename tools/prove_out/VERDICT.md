# Coherence and sector: the verdict

**Coherence: NOT PROVABLE for this readout; keep off the website.**
The example `0.847` has no identified calculation, input record or scale in this
repository. We cannot explain that particular number to a customer or claim
it measures reliability, understanding, health or a physical device.

Several calculations share the name coherence. One combines two supplied
progress fractions into a score that rises when either input rises. Another
measures closeness between numerical positions in addresses. These quantities
use different scales. Their passing tests do not establish a confidence measure.

**Sector: PROVED as an address label; keep off the website.**
For addresses in the tested valid ranges, it is one of eight groups selected
by a number in the address, called theta. Crossing a range boundary changes
the group; changing other address fields does not. The address format matters,
and moving between formats can change the label.

A customer explanation could be: "This label groups addresses by a number in
them. It does not analyze your words or give another agent permission."

RADIX has not approved displaying these generic readouts. That decision is
unchanged. Special address values and rules about which groups are neighbors
differ between implementations; those cases cannot inherit the tested label
result. Consent checks remain separate.

A shared coherence scale or common treatment of those special cases needs an
explicit proposal and owner decision. No such change was applied.
The [criteria](VERDICT_CRITERIA.md) and [validation](CX017_VALIDATION.md) give
the precise domains, counterexamples, commands, counts and reproduction limits.
