## IMPOSED policy choices and limits

The complete contract and commands are in [the crate README](../../../rust/rpp-steer/README.md).
Control/billboard rank loss, RTT, jitter, cost, then ID. Bulk ranks measured kbps
descending before that same order. Throughput is never inferred from RTT or MTU.
Zero throughput is bulk-ineligible. Cost order is Unmetered, Metered, ControlOnly.
BLE remains control-only regardless of its label; billboards never use BLE.

Callers explicitly choose hold ticks and absolute control/billboard ppm or bulk
kbps margins; Hysteresis has no Default. Both elapsed hold and margin must be met
at >= boundaries. Secondary-only improvements require zero margin. All arithmetic
uses comparisons/subtraction, including u64 endpoints. Missing/down/too-small/
newly forbidden incumbents bypass holds immediately; no-channel clears that class.
Malformed banks and rollback leave every state and the clock unchanged. Future
observations are rejected; observation expiry is left to caller policy.

MTU means ONE atomic underlay payload. The BLE-only fixture selects 20-byte
fragment-sized control chunks and rejects a 112-byte request at MTU 20. It does
not integrate fragmentation or claim a full control envelope fits a notification.
Readings are scripted inputs, not measured radio performance. Selection supplies
no consent, membership, tunnel verification, independent path proof or retry grant.
