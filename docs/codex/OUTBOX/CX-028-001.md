## Declared reservation profiles

The existing safe probe now contains named, compile-time RISC-V assertions.
Each fixed cap is its original baseline plus 10%, rounded upward; caps do not
move with observed object sizes. Equality passes; cap+1 fails its named assertion.

| Independent profile | Declared reservation | Baseline | Fixed cap |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Billboard encoding | Two 925-byte buffers: codec scratch and caller output | 1850 | 2035 |
| Control fragmentation | Reassembler<4,112> 1080 + Fragmenter 20 + caller wire buffer 20 | 1120 | 1232 |
| Steering | Selector 88 + Candidate[16] 768 | 856 | 942 |

These are scoped stack/static reservations according to caller placement.
They exclude compiler/callee/verifier frames, temporaries, interrupts, linker/BSS,
allocator overhead, firmware stack/heap and board measurements. Do not sum the
three independent scenarios into a whole-application bound. The 20-byte wire
buffer is a declared caller profile, not a general MTU maximum.

Known later reassembler1144 and selector96 sizes fit the original caps. No cap
was raised and no integrated future-type source tree was claimed tested here.
All eight original diagnostic globals and the entire product Rust tree remain
unchanged. The new assertions are in the standalone compile-only probe.
